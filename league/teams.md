# THE SPINDLE LEAGUE — Major League, Current Season

*32 franchises. One bell. No clock. The Long Season is 33 rigs; the
top 16 cross into **the Jump** (the interstellar single-elimination
playoff). We are at the eve of the Jump. Records below are final
regular-season.*

Every franchise's *style* is dictated by its home habitat's *physics*
(see `design/sport.md` §6): big slow cylinders breed patient **Fall**
power; small fast stations breed **Rise** chaos; mid cylinders breed the
**ground**-and-line broker game. Style is not chosen. Style is *spun in.*

Faction key: **FRM** Frontier Republic of Michigan · **CNE**
Commonwealth of New England · **CON** the Concordat · **BLT** Belt
independents · **EUR** Silesia/Old-Europe diaspora · **REF** the
Concordat-kept governing body.

## SOL CONFERENCE — "First Spin" (12)
*The old cylinders of Sol. The most cylinders, the oldest money, all
three Sol powers in one sky. Sol hosts the league office.*

### FRM Sol bloc — the Fall dynasties

**1. Tuebor Spin Detroiters** · *Tuebor*, the great slow Frontier
agricultural cylinder (8 km, shallow slope, gentle hand)
- FRM · Colors: works-green & oxide-orange · Logo: a clenched line-claw
  over the Michigan motto **TUEBOR** ("I will defend")
- Style: **Fall power**, league archetype. Long casts, a granite Anchor,
  the honest 2 ground out a hundred times.
- **Record 27–6 · #1 overall seed.** The sport's Yankees-and-dynasty.
  Neutrals are bored by them; *Tuebor* doesn't care, *Tuebor* spins on.
  Fanbase: three generations deep, owns the calm.

**2. Cascade Works Foremen** · a Sol heavy-industry cylinder
- FRM · gunmetal & safety-yellow · Logo: a torque wrench bent into a ring
- Style: Fall, blue-collar, **clatter**-heavy defense. They make your
  bell ring ugly.
- **Record 21–12 · #7 seed.** Fanbase: actual riggers and yard crews;
  the loudest rim-band seats in the league.

**3. Saginaw Bell** · mid-old Sol cylinder
- FRM · cream & maroon · Logo: a tuning-fork bell
- Style: Fall, tempo-control, the most beautiful *clean* passing in Sol.
- **Record 18–15 · #12 seed.** The connoisseur's team. Bell rings true
  more than any club; scores least dramatically.

**4. Veracity Yard Probes** · the Sol shipyard cylinder that built the
*Veracity* recovery probe
- FRM · deep-space black & telemetry-cyan · Logo: a probe silhouette on
  a parabolic line
- Style: Fall base, sneaky **Loop** specialists — engineers who do the
  Coriolis math for fun.
- **Record 16–17 · missed the Jump (17th).** Heartbreak franchise; lost
  a play-in on a clattered bell. Fanbase: shipwrights, the FASTPITCH
  faithful, gallows humor.

### CNE Sol bloc — the line game

**5. Boston Commons Reach** · balanced CNE flagship cylinder
- CNE · revolutionary-blue & cream · Logo: a goal ring over a cobble
- Style: **ground & line**, tempo, away-points. The Commonwealth's
  pride; FRM's friendly twin-league nemesis.
- **Record 24–9 · #2 overall seed.** The great rivalry of the sport is
  *Tuebor vs. Commons* — Fall dynasty vs. line craft, Frontier vs.
  Commonwealth, exactly the canon twin-league tension with a bell in it.

**6. Plymouth Counter** · CNE mid cylinder
- CNE · slate & harvest-gold · Logo: a counterweight on a plumb line
- Style: line, patient, brutal Reach play.
- **Record 20–13 · #9 seed.** Fanbase: old Commonwealth money, very
  smug, deservedly.

**7. New Haven Ear** · CNE cylinder, home of the league's officiating
academy
- CNE · ivy-green & chalk-white · Logo: a blindfolded ear inside a ring
- Style: line, *legalist* — they win the Ear's calls because they
  literally train the Ears. Everyone hates this and it is canon.
- **Record 19–14 · #10 seed.**

**8. Manchester Memorial** · CNE cylinder named for the lost colony ship
*Manchester* (destroyed by sabotage in Wolf 359)
- CNE · mourning-violet & steel · Logo: a broken ring, mended with a line
- Style: defiant **ground** rig; a franchise that exists as an act of
  remembrance. Every match opens with a silent bell.
- **Record 22–11 · #5 seed.** The league's emotional heart. When
  *Manchester Memorial* makes a run, even Frontier crowds stand. NISO
  has *opinions* about their security detail.

### Concordat Sol bloc — the brokers

**9. Roma Aeterna Brokers** · the Concordat's flagship Sol cylinder
(Rome-built, balanced)
- CON · Tyrian purple & Lira-gold · Logo: a she-wolf curled into a ring,
  Vatican key crossed with a line
- Style: **ground**, away-draws, the infuriating broker rig — wins
  trophies on the road nobody enjoys watching.
- **Record 23–10 · #3 overall seed.** Fanbase: cosmopolitan, multilingual,
  shows up *everywhere* (the Lira travels). Quietly the most-followed
  club across non-aligned space.

**10. Vatican Spindle** · the certification body's ceremonial home club
- CON/REF · white & gold · Logo: the crossed keys over the reference arc
- Style: ground, ceremonial, plays the negotiated reference spin —
  *nobody's* home conditions, on purpose.
- **Record 17–16 · missed the Jump (16th, lost play-in).** Exists more
  as institution than contender. The joke writes itself; the joke is
  canon; the joke is load-bearing, exactly like the Vatican-guaranteed
  Lira.

**11. Lira Free Company** · no fixed cylinder — a Concordat barnstorming
franchise that rents calm wherever the Lira runs
- CON · mercenary-grey & gold · Logo: a coin on a line, no home ring
- Style: adaptive ground; the only club with *no home advantage and no
  home disadvantage*, by design.
- **Record 20–13 · #8 seed.** Belt operators and frontier outposts adopt
  them as a flag of convenience. A genuinely strange, beloved team.

**12. Trieste Counterweight** · Concordat secondary Sol cylinder
- CON · adriatic-teal & rust · Logo: a balance scale as two rings
- Style: ground, defensive, dull, effective.
- **Record 15–18 · missed the Jump.** The Concordat's farm of brokers.

## THE NEAR REACH — "First Light" (10)
*The first interstellar colonies. Where rig stopped being a Sol game.*

**13. Toliman Gradient** · Alpha Centauri B (Toliman), a small fast
Frontier spin-station — savage slope, vicious hand
- FRM · binary-white & furnace-red · Logo: two suns through a ring,
  a Coriolis spiral tail
- Style: **Rise chaos**, all Freewing, the most exciting and least
  stable rig in the league. They chase 5s and 7s and live and die on it.
- **Record 25–8 · #4 overall seed.** The franchise that *proved the
  colonies could beat Sol*. Toliman's Freewing is the most famous rigger
  alive. Fanbase: feral, sleepless (binary day), evangelical.

**14. Centauri b Hammerline** · Alpha Cen colony cylinder, mid-fast
- FRM · ochre & black · Logo: a hammer whose head is a line-claw
- Style: Rise-leaning power, the "hammer" — they win 7s by force.
- **Record 21–12 · #6 seed.** The colony that out-produces the
  Concordat; the team plays like it knows that.

**15. Proxima Long Shift** · Proxima outpost, a brutal little station
- FRM · red-dwarf crimson & dark · Logo: a single dim sun, a long line
- Style: Rise, exhausted, magnificent. Plays like the work shift that
  invented the game never ended.
- **Record 17–16 · missed the Jump (15th).** Frontier romance incarnate.

**16. Fomalhaut Dagon Wake** · CNE Fomalhaut colony cylinder, in sight
of the dead world Dagon (RKV-killed, ~1870 CE)
- CNE · ash-grey & deep-red · Logo: a cracked planet behind a ring
- Style: haunted **ground** rig. A franchise that plays under the
  knowledge of what a relativistic impactor did to a living world.
- **Record 19–14 · #11 seed.** NISO does not love that the team's
  iconography keeps the Dagon question in front of forty thousand people
  a night. The fanbase doesn't care. *Especially* doesn't care.

**17. Hebat Resolve** · CNE Hebat (Fomalhaut), the colony hit by suicide
bombers
- CNE · scar-white & iron · Logo: a ring scored by a line that didn't
  break
- Style: ground, defiant, low-scoring, beloved. Sister-grief franchise
  to *Manchester Memorial*; their meetings are the most-listened rig in
  human space.
- **Record 22–11 · #6-seed-adjacent → #13 seed** (tiebreak loss to
  Hammerline). Ananke considers their spindle a target. The league does
  not discuss it. NISO *very much* does.

**18. Wolf 359 Migration** · CNE Wolf 359, near Tartarus (the cryo gas
giant with bird-migration-patterned turbulence)
- CNE · storm-violet & pale-gold · Logo: a skein of migrating birds
  bent into a Coriolis arc
- Style: Rise, flocking line-work — the most *coordinated* high game in
  the league; whole crews move like the thing in Tartarus's clouds.
- **Record 20–13 · #14 seed.** Fanbase obsessed with the macrofauna
  question. The team leans all the way into it. CNE finds this useful
  for morale and unsettling for security.

**19. Gliese 65 Twinstar** · CNE binary-system colony
- CNE · twin-amber & navy · Logo: two rings interlocked
- Style: balanced line.
- **Record 18–15 · missed the Jump.**

**20. Lacaille Longjump** · CNE deep-ish colony, named for the jump that
reaches it
- CNE · void-black & signal-orange · Logo: a dotted jump-arc through a ring
- Style: ground, travel-hardened (they're always the away team).
- **Record 16–17 · missed the Jump.**

**21. Tau Ceti Mare Nostrum** · the Concordat's flagship colony — the
new Mediterranean, a balanced mid cylinder
- CON · sea-blue & gold · Logo: a wave curling into a ring, **MARE
  NOSTRUM**
- Style: the perfected **ground/line broker** game. The Concordat's
  *actual* contender, not just its institution.
- **Record 24–9 · #4-seed-adjacent → #5 overall (tiebreak vs Toliman) →
  seed #4.** Plays for the away point and the draw and wins the Jump
  doing it, every few years, while everyone complains. *This is the
  Concordat's whole foreign policy as a sport.*

**22. 82 Eridani Secondi** · Concordat secondary colony
- CON · terracotta & gold · Logo: a small ring inside a larger one
- Style: ground, junior-broker.
- **Record 15–18 · missed the Jump.** The Concordat's far farm.

interstellar distance to play you count less than wins earned on the

### First-round storylines (the ones the calls will sell)
- **1 Tuebor v 16 Vesta** — the dynasty vs. the origin myth. "We defend"
  vs. "we threw the first loop." Whoever loses, the marketing wins.
- **2 Commons v 15 Wolf 359** — line discipline vs. the flock. CNE
  civil war; the Tartarus-macrofauna fanbase makes it eerie.
- **5 Manchester v 12 Belt Free Local 9** — the grief franchise vs. the
  dual-use talent fountain. NISO has people in both rim-bands. This is a
  *lead waiting to happen.*
- **6 Luhman Orbital Riot v 11 Cascade Works** — the riot vs. the
  foremen. Labor rig, both ends. France-backed-riot optics on national
  audio. Kanzo can light this fuse anytime.
- **8 Toliman v 9 Hebat** — chaos vs. grief, the two best low seeds,
  the connoisseur's pick for rig of the year.

## OFF THE BOARD — canon hooks the league won't print

- **The Directorate Circuit** — France's breakaway pariah league.
  O-drive-funded, an aesthetic of *forced Rises* the Spindle League
  calls decadent and dangerous. Doesn't recognize the Concordat-kept
  reference; runs its own spin. Recognition of the Circuit is a
  diplomatic act. A rigger who jumps to the Circuit is a *defection.*
  Pure GM antagonist surface.
- **The Ananke question** — Ananke has tried for a championship spindle
  once. The league does not discuss it. NISO does not either. The reader
  is told only this much, on purpose. (17776's witnessing-narrator
  restraint, applied as a plot device.)
- **The talent pipeline** — rig academy → astronautics corps → (for a
  quiet few) France or Ananke. The best Freewings think in Coriolis the
  way aggies used to think in everything. The league's unspoken anxiety
  is the campaign's, with a jersey on.
- **Spindle certification as recognition** — when a new colony gets a
  certified regulation spindle, it has been told it *belongs.* When one
  loses certification (sabotage, riot, neglect), a faction has lost a
  civic organ on interstellar audio. Every certification is a small
  treaty. Every sabotaged spindle is a bloodless humiliation. Hand to GM.
