# RIG vs AXBALL — Comparison

**Status, stated plainly:** Kanzo's *AXBALL: The Sourcebook* is the GM's
own realization, marked "provisional canon pending ratification." It is
**canon**. *RIG* (this repo) is an independent sibling derivation built
in parallel without sight of AXBALL. This document compares them and
recommends how RIG should serve AXBALL — not compete with it. (Per
standing player doctrine: don't crowd the GM; lead with analysis; offer,
don't argue.)

---

## 1. The headline: independent convergence

Two people, same canon + same physics, no contact, **landed on the same
sport.** That is the most important finding here — it means the design
space has a true shape and AXBALL is sitting on it. The overlaps are not
vague vibes; they are specific, load-bearing, and arrived at
independently:

Tell Kanzo this first. The convergence is the validation: AXBALL didn't

| Axis | AXBALL (canon) | RIG (sibling) | Verdict |

| Asymmetry | Coriolis is **skill-only**; goals symmetric | goals **structurally** asymmetric (Faith cheap / Free dear) | **RIG has something here — see §3** |

The divergences are not "AXBALL is wrong about X." On every pure
mechanics fork, AXBALL is the better-specified and canonical call. RIG's
value is not its rules; it is **four additive ideas that don't conflict
with AXBALL.**

## 3. What RIG can *contribute* to AXBALL (offer, don't argue)

Four things, ranked by value, each canon-compatible — i.e. addable to
AXBALL without contradicting a word of the sourcebook:

1. **Structural goal asymmetry (highest value).** AXBALL's own thesis is
   that "spin exploitation is the subtlest art of the sport," yet its
   Gates are symmetric — the asymmetry is purely skill expression. RIG's
   one genuinely original mechanic: make the two Gates *worth or behave
   differently* by spin direction (a spinward Gate vs an antispinward
   Gate). This deepens AXBALL's *existing* central theme, costs nothing,
   contradicts nothing, and turns "which end do we defend" into a real
   decision. **This is the one to pitch Kanzo.**
2. **The Concordat as the neutral keeper.** AXBALL holds the Fullspin at
   "a neutral site (Gateway Station)" but never says *who guarantees
   neutrality*. Our player-faction, the Concordat — army-less,
   currency-trusted, Vatican-Lira-guaranteed — is the perfect
   institutional answer: the IAL's neutral certification/reference is
   Concordat-kept, exactly as the Lira is. Fills an unspecified gap with
   a faction Kanzo already owns, and gives the Concordat a canonical
   role in his institution. Pure gift.
3. **The chime → why the media economy is audio.** AXBALL's broadcast
   economy ("courier video, no spoilers") works, but a *silent* Stone
   leaves "why is the delayed broadcast culturally central" slightly
   under-motivated. RIG's chiming ball makes the sport legible by ear,
   which independently *explains* an audio-first interstellar economy.
   Offer the chime as optional Stone-flavor that strengthens AXBALL's
   own economic thesis.
4. **The closed-Coriolis "loop" as a named prestige score.** AXBALL's
   3-point Long Launch is the prestige score; RIG's 7-point Loop is a
   *closed* Coriolis orbit (untouched, curves back through the Gate).
   Offer it as an optional rare apex of the Long Launch family — the
   "perfect game" of AXBALL — not a replacement.

Everything else RIG has (no-clock purism, grapple, 6 positions, the
calm/skin geometry) is a *variant aesthetic*, not a contribution. Hold
it as a "house-rules / pickup AXBALL" sibling, not a proposal.

## 4. Decisions taken from this comparison

- **The playable game now implements AXBALL canon**, not RIG: the Lane,
  the Stone, Gates, Frames + High Frames + Free Spin, push-off +
  thrumblers, Long Launch scoring, 7 (arcade-reduced) roles. The
  physics is identical in both, so nothing in the engine is wasted.
  The swarm was briefed on AXBALL canon.
- **The RIG dossier stays** as the sibling artifact + the carrier of the
  four contributions above. It is explicitly *not* presented to Kanzo as
  a rival sourcebook.
- **Recommended package to Kanzo:** (a) "independent convergence — your
  AXBALL is on the attractor, here's the proof"; (b) the four additive
  proposals in §3, framed as steal-if-useful, especially #1 and #2;
  (c) the playable AXBALL match as a toy that makes his canon tangible.
